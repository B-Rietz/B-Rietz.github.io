Also at Lennar

Across three teams

Dashboards for 500+ sales users

The 500+ New Home Consultants, sales managers, and others in sales who used our CRM Analytics dashboards didn't trust numbers that didn't match what they expected, and struggled to find answers. As one of our subject-matter experts, I watched consultants use them, corrected the calculations, and reworked filters and layout with our product owner, from broad reporting down to a dashboard built for six divisions. Managers now use them in team meetings and reviews.

I also built a component so Area Sales Managers log each consultant's weekly sales plan right from the dashboard they already review. Those plans had never been tracked before. It's in production.

2

An Agentforce bot for Internet Sales Consultants

On the ISC Experience team, I built an Agentforce AI agent for the consultants who handle online buyers. It handles their common issues and briefs them on recent releases, so answers come to them instead of the other way around. It hasn't launched yet. I also supported the wider ISC experience.

Keeping the platform healthy
